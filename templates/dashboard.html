How to interpret these results

  • Display, click, and report event totals are stored events; they are not the same as distinct participants.
  • Rates use distinct participants with a displayed email as the denominator; a zero denominator produces a 0.0% rate.
  • Module 3 deduplicates each action for a participant and sample within a campaign. Browser sessions are synthetic IDs, not verified people.
  • These measures describe this small local training dataset and are not estimates of real-world susceptibility or organizational risk.
  • Dashboard labels describe the exercise dataset. They are not output from the future Module 5 detection engine.
